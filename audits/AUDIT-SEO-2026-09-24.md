## 0. Verdict up front

- **Traffic is flat because nothing that moves it has changed.** 514 clicks / 9,594 impressions in the last 28 days vs 503 / 10,164 the 28 before. Weekly non-branded clicks have bounced between 5 and 17 for 16 straight weeks with no trend (section 3.1). Non-branded CTR is 1.4%, exactly what it was on 07-07 and 08-29.
- **The site is still not the bottleneck.** 39 of 43 sitemap URLs are indexed, every redirect and header check passes, Lighthouse is 90 to 99 on every page except one fixable layout shift on `/specials`, schema and llms.txt are in place, and Bing already has the new pages via IndexNow. The four Claude-side fixes in section 6 are worth doing but none of them will move the click line on their own.
- **The lever is still Google Business Profile, and every GBP item from July is still open.** This pull has direct evidence: the local pack payload for "breakfast catering near me" returns Copperline's website button as `http://copperlineeatery.com/`, and the http row now takes 83% of homepage clicks (367 of 444), up from 75%. The listing still carries the "accepts reservations" attribute the site contradicts. No posting cadence exists (Tier C never started). Review velocity is about half of Petros: +14 reviews vs +27 in the same 26 days.
- **The http row is the pack.** 13 of the 15 "breakfast near me" clicks this month arrived on the http homepage row on mobile at position 14.5, which is the GBP website button, not an organic blue link. So the flat http row is a flat count of people tapping "Website" on the listing, and the pack is where Copperline is absent from anywhere but its own block (section 4).
- **The 12-week content gate (set 08-29) is not met yet, and it is too early to call.** Non-branded clicks went 43 -> 51, `/specials` has 0 clicks on 212 impressions at position 5.5, and two of the four blog posts are not indexed yet. Tier A and B are working mechanically (weekly board published 3 of 3 weeks, the Monday cron opened a PR 4 of 4 weeks) but Tier C, the part expected to move numbers, has no evidence of having started.
- **Single most valuable action:** Dad spends 5 minutes in GBP (https website field, clear reservations, post the 09-19 board photo with a link to `/specials`). Everything else in this audit is second-order.

AI Overviews are intermittent on these local queries, so there is no citation to check yet. Recommendation: fold the 4-query pull into the monthly re-measure as a tripwire rather than build anything for it.

What is stale: `llms.txt` was last edited 2026-07-27. It does not mention `/specials` (or the archive), does not mention `/blog` or any post, and says the Catering page has "packages, pricing" when `/catering` is deliberately price-free (the quote builder has the pricing). Hours, address, phone, rating and the catering facts still match `restaurant.ts`. Minor: the schema `reviewCount` is 1130 while Google shows 1,120; keep it at or under the live count (Google does not show self-serving `aggregateRating` rich results for LocalBusiness anyway, so this is hygiene, not a ranking item).
