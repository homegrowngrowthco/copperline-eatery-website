[Bright Nights at Forest Park](https://explorewesternmass.com/event/bright-nights-at-forest-park-2/) runs from Tuesday, December 1 through Sunday, January 3 at Forest Park, 300 Sumner Avenue. It is a three-mile drive-thru display of over 750,000 lights with festive scenes, iconic characters and gardens, plus Santa's Magical Forest with snacks, souvenirs and a carousel.

If the lights are on your family's plans this month, let us take care of a meal that same day. We serve breakfast all day, right up until closing, so a lunch stop can still be pancakes and eggs.

## Bringing the kids

Our Kids' Breakfast is $7.50 for children under 12. They choose one egg and toast, two French toast, or two pancakes, with bacon, sausage, or ham, and milk or juice is included. We have high chairs and booster seats, and crayons and coloring sheets at the table. A Hot Chocolate is $2.95 and comes with one refill.

For the grown-ups, the Copperline Special ($12.25) is three eggs, two pancakes, homefries and toast. The Huck's Special ($7.25) is two pancakes with two eggs. At lunch, The Pilgrim ($11.95) is turkey with stuffing and cranberry sauce on a water roll.

We don't take reservations. If you are coming with eight or more, call us at (413) 594-8332 ahead of time so we can prepare seating. Saturday and Sunday mornings between 8 and 11 are usually our busiest, so arriving before 8 or after 11:30 tends to mean a shorter wait.

## Also this week

On Wednesday, December 9 at 7 p.m., the Chicopee Community Band presents [Fantasia for Christmas](https://www.artshubwma.org/event/fantasia-for-christmas-chicopee-community-band-christmas-concert), a free concert of Christmas tunes at the Stanley F. Kozikowski Auditorium at Chicopee Comprehensive High School, 617 Montgomery Street.

On Sunday, December 13, the Springfield Museums at 21 Edwards Street open early, from 9 to 11 a.m., for [Sensory Friendly Sunday](https://springfieldmuseums.org/organizer/family-programs/). We open at 7 a.m. on Sundays, so breakfast with us can come first.

We hope to see you this season.
