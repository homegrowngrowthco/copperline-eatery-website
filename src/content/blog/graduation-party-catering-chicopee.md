## Why a Breakfast or Brunch Party Works So Well

Morning and midday parties are genuinely easier to pull off than evening ones. People are in a good mood, the weather is usually more forgiving in June, and the cost per person tends to be lower than a dinner spread. A late-morning graduation party also gives families the rest of the afternoon free for whatever comes next.

A breakfast buffet travels well, covers picky eaters and big appetites alike, and lets guests serve themselves at their own pace instead of waiting in a single line.

## The Buffet Options

The catering menu gives you several ways to structure the spread depending on your headcount and what your crowd expects.

The standard Buffet Packages run from Buffet Package #1 through Buffet Package #5, starting at $13.95 per person. Each tier builds on the last. For a straightforward party where people want eggs, homefries, and something hot to eat, the lower packages cover the ground. If your group wants more variety, the higher-numbered packages and the Hot Item Buffet give you more to work with.

The Specialty Buffets step things up a bit. The Italian Buffet is a good option if you're mixing a graduation brunch with a later afternoon crowd that expects something more substantial. Specialty Buffet pricing starts at $14.95 per person and goes up from there depending on what you choose.

Per-person pricing makes it straightforward to estimate your total once you have a headcount. A party of 30 on a mid-range package lands in a range most families find very workable compared to outside catering or restaurant buyouts.

## A Few Things to Sort Out Early

Catering quantities are built around headcount, so even a rough number helps when you request a quote. Err on the side of a few extra rather than cutting it close. Copperline is a breakfast and lunch operation, so the catering is calibrated for morning and midday service. If your party starts at 10 a.m. or runs through early afternoon, the timing lines up well. If you have guests with dietary preferences, mention that when you reach out. The kitchen can often work with you. Confirm pickup or delivery logistics when you place your order so there are no surprises on the day.
