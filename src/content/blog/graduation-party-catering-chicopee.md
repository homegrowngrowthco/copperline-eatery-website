## Why a Buffet Makes Sense for a Grad Party

Grad parties are casual by nature. People are coming and going, kids are running around, and nobody wants to sit down for a plated meal. A buffet fits that rhythm. Guests grab what they want, go back for seconds, and you're not stuck timing entrées around a crowd that shows up whenever they feel like it.

At The Copperline Eatery, our catering packages are built exactly for that kind of gathering. We keep it simple and we keep it real: good food, generous portions, and options that work for a wide range of tastes.

## Planning Around Your Headcount

The earlier you reach out, the easier this goes. Graduation parties in Chicopee, Ludlow, Holyoke, and Springfield all tend to cluster in the same few weeks in May and June. We work with you on timing and quantity, but we need a reasonable heads-up to do it right.

A few things to think through before you call or fill out a quote request:

- Roughly how many people are you expecting? Even a ballpark number helps us recommend the right package tier.
- Is this an outdoor backyard setup or an indoor venue? That sometimes affects what works best logistically.
- Do you have any guests with dietary restrictions? We can talk through what fits.
- What time are you expecting the bulk of your guests to arrive? That shapes how we time everything.
