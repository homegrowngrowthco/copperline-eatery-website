Planning breakfast for a crowd is different from planning lunch or dinner. People tend to eat a bit lighter in the morning, but the logistics still require careful thought, especially when you need hot food ready at a specific time. The questions we hear most often come down to three things: how much food, how much lead time, and how much will it cost?

## How Much Food Per Person

A standard breakfast spread typically covers eggs, bacon and sausage, home fries, and a bread option like French toast or pancakes. For a buffet-style setup, a good working estimate is one to one and a half portions per adult for most of those items. Children generally eat less, so if your guest list skews younger, adjust down a bit.

For a group of 25, quantities are small enough that individual portions can work as well as a buffet, which keeps waste low. For 50 or 100 guests, a buffet-style or to-go buffet format is usually more practical. We offer on-site service, hot drop-off, or pick-up at the restaurant, so the format can fit your venue and your schedule.

## Lead Time by Group Size

For most events, we ask for one to two weeks of notice. If your event falls on a weekend or involves a larger group, plan for three to four weeks. The earlier you reach out, the more options you have on timing and menu.

## What to Tell Us When You Call

To put together an accurate quote, it helps to have your event date and start time, your approximate guest count (broken down by adults and children under 12 if relevant), your preferred service format, and any dietary needs in your group. For children under 12, we offer a dedicated kids option and are happy to discuss how that fits into a catering setup when you call.
