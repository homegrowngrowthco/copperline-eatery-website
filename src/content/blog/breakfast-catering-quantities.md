Planning a catered breakfast sounds simple until you're actually doing it. How many trays? How much food per person? What happens if half your guests go back for seconds? Work through the quantities and costs before you call us and you'll be in much better shape.

## Start With the Per-Person Formula

For a buffet-style breakfast, plan for roughly one and a half servings per adult. People eat more when food is plentiful and visible, especially at morning events where coffee is flowing and the mood is relaxed. For a tighter office drop-off or a quick before-meeting spread, one serving per person is usually enough.

Our catering packages are priced per person, which makes the math straightforward. Buffet Package #1 and Buffet Package #2 start at $13.95 per person and step up through $14.95 and $15.95 depending on what you want included. For a broader selection with more specialty items, the Choice Buffet #1 comes in at $14.95 per person and the Choice Buffet #2 at $17.95.

## Estimating for 25 People

A group of 25 covers most office breakfasts, small team meetings, bridal showers, and neighborhood events. This size is manageable.

For a straightforward spread, start with the lower-tier packages and plan for one serving each with a small buffer. At $13.95 per person, feeding 25 guests runs roughly $349 before any add-ons. Step up to the Hot Item Buffet at $15.95 per person and you're looking at around $399 for the group. For 25 people, one setup station is usually enough. Just keep traffic flow in mind if the space is tight.

## Estimating for 50 People

Fifty guests is where planning gets a little more serious. This is the size of a typical company all-hands, a school event, or a mid-size family reunion breakfast.

At this count, build in that one-and-a-half servings buffer, particularly if your event runs longer than an hour. People circle back. Budget using the per-person price multiplied by 60 to 65 equivalent servings rather than a strict 50. For the Choice Buffet #2 at $17.95 per person, feeding 50 guests at face value is around $898. Factoring in a modest buffer brings your planning estimate closer to $1,000 to $1,075. That is your own planning math, not a quoted price. Always confirm directly with us for an accurate number.

For 50 people, think about whether you want two serving stations or a longer single line with multiple trays per item to avoid bottlenecks.

## A Few Things That Affect Your Order

**Event length.** A 45-minute working breakfast needs less food than a two-hour open-house brunch. The longer the window, the more you should build in.

**Guest mix.** A group eating a real meal before a long day will eat more than a group stopping by a brief morning reception.

**Sides and extras.** Our packages include different combinations of items. If your crowd expects multiple protein options, look at the higher-tier packages rather than adding individual trays.

**Kids.** Children typically eat less than adults, which can offset some of the buffer you'd otherwise add.
