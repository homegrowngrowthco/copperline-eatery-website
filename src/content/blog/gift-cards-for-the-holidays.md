Yes, we sell gift cards, and we can make one out for any amount you like. There's no minimum and no maximum.

## How to get one

Gift cards are picked up in person at the restaurant, at 409 Broadway in Chicopee. Stop in and ask any of us while we're open, or call us ahead at (413) 594-8332 and then pick the card up in person. We don't sell them online.

## How they work

- **They never expire.** Use it next week or next year.
- **Dine-in and takeout.** A gift card works when you eat with us and when you pick up a takeout order.
- **Not for catering.** Gift cards can't be used on catering orders.
- **Not on DoorDash.** Orders placed through DoorDash can't be paid with one of our gift cards.
- **No reloading.** An existing card can't have more money added to it. If you want to give more, pick up a new card.

Birthdays and thank-yous come around all year, so a gift card can be handy well after the holidays. Since 1993 we've been a family-owned diner here in Chicopee, and we're always glad when someone sends a friend or a relative our way.

Thank you for thinking of our small, family-owned restaurant when you're picking out a gift.
