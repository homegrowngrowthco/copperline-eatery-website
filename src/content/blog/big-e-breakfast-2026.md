The Big E runs September 18 through October 4 in West Springfield, about 15 minutes from our door at 409 Broadway, Chicopee. Before you spend the day on your feet, stop in and eat a real breakfast.

## Our Hours

We open at 6 a.m. Monday through Saturday and at 7 a.m. on Sunday. We close at 2 p.m. on weekdays, 1:30 p.m. on Saturdays, and 1 p.m. on Sundays. We do not take reservations, so just come in.

## Something Filling Before a Long Day

The Copperline Special with Bacon or Sausage includes three eggs, two pancakes, homefries, toast, and your choice of bacon or sausage. The Eggs Benedict comes with two poached eggs on English muffin halves with Canadian bacon and our house-made hollandaise sauce, served with homefries. We have also won awards for our breakfast and our French toast.

The Hash Breakfast comes with our homemade corned beef hash, two eggs, and toast. The hash is something we are known for.

## A Note on French Toast

The Banana Bread French Toast is made from our homemade banana bread and is a good pick on a cool September or October morning.

## Bringing the Kids

We have high chairs and booster seats available. Kids under 12 can order from our children's menu. The Kids' Breakfast is $7.50 and includes a choice of one egg and toast, two French toast, or two pancakes, with sausage, bacon, or ham, plus milk or juice. The Huck's Special comes with two pancakes and two eggs and works well for smaller appetites.

## Find Us

We are at 409 Broadway, Chicopee, MA 01020. You can reach us at (413) 594-8332.
