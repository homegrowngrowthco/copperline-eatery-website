For most office meetings, order breakfast one to two weeks ahead. If the meeting is a large one, or it falls on a weekend, give us three to four weeks when you can.

Corporate meetings and office lunches are among the events we cater most. If your meeting is closer than a week away, call us at (413) 594-8332 to check availability.

## What we need for a quote

We price breakfast catering for each event. Once we know a few things, we can give you a per-person price:

- **Headcount:** tell us how many people, and we size the order for you as part of the quote. There is no minimum and no maximum.
- **Menu:** our breakfast catering includes eggs, bacon and sausage, home fries, French toast and pancakes.
- **Service:** you can pick it up at the restaurant, we can drop it off hot, or we can serve on site. Hot drop-off can be as early as 5 a.m., there is no delivery fee, and there is no set distance limit.

When you reach out, send the date, headcount and start time.

We can set breakfast out buffet style, pack it as a to-go buffet, or do individual portions.

If you would like a number before you talk to us, our [quote builder](/catering/quote) puts together an itemized estimate in about a minute. You can also call, and we will price it with you.
