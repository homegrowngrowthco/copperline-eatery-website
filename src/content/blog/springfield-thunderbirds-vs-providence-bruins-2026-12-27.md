The [Springfield Thunderbirds vs. Providence Bruins](https://springfieldthunderbirds.com/news/t-birds-roll-out-2026-27-schedule) game is Sunday, December 27, at the MassMutual Center in Springfield. It's a Sunday afternoon AHL home game for the Thunderbirds, and it starts at 3:05 p.m.

An afternoon start leaves the morning free, so it's a good day for a late breakfast or an early lunch with us before you head to the game. We close at 1 p.m. on Sundays, so leave yourself time to sit down and eat.

Weekend mornings between 8 and 11 are usually our busiest stretch. For a shorter wait, come in before 8 or after 11:30. Tables are first-come, first-serve. If you're bringing a group of eight or more, please call us at (413) 594-8332 ahead of time so we can get seating ready.

Breakfast is served all day, and lunch is on the menu whenever we're open, so everybody at the table can order what they're hungry for. The Hash Breakfast ($11.50) is our homemade corned beef hash with two eggs and toast. The Copperline Special ($12.25) is three eggs, two pancakes, homefries and toast. From the lunch side, there's the Reuben ($12.95).

Bringing the kids? The Kids' Breakfast ($7.50) is for guests under 12: one egg and toast, two French toast, or two pancakes, with bacon, sausage or ham, plus milk or juice. We have high chairs and booster seats, too.

Enjoy the game, and we hope to see you that morning.
