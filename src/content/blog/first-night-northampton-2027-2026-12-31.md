First Night Northampton starts at noon on New Year's Eve this year. Here is how a meal with us fits around it.

## What is happening

[First Night Northampton 2027](https://www.northamptonartscouncil.org/news/celebrate-the-arts-first-night-northampton-2027-is-coming) is Northampton's alcohol-free New Year's Eve arts festival. It is on Thursday, December 31, and starts at noon in downtown Northampton, with over 100 performances across more than 20 downtown venues.

## A late breakfast before noon

We are open our regular hours on Thursday, December 31, 6 a.m. to 2 p.m., and breakfast is served all day. A late morning stop works just as well for eggs and pancakes as it does for a sandwich. If you want to be in Northampton when things begin at noon, come in early enough to eat and get on the road.

For a big plate before a long day, the Copperline Special ($12.25) is three eggs, two pancakes, homefries, and toast. The Hash Breakfast ($11.50) is our homemade corned beef hash with two eggs and toast. The Eggs Benedict ($11.50) comes with homefries.

We do not take reservations, so seating is first come, first serve. If your group is eight or more, call us at (413) 594-8332 ahead of time so we can get seating ready.

## Something to take along

Takeout runs as usual on December 31, so call that same number if you would like food for the drive or for later. Most orders are ready in under 30 minutes. At lunch there is the Reuben ($12.95) and the Turkey Club ($13.25), and breakfast is available to go as well.

We hope your last day of 2026 is a happy one, and we would be glad to see you on your way.
