First Night Northampton starts at noon on New Year's Eve this year. Here is how a meal with us fits around it.

## What is happening

[First Night Northampton 2027](https://www.northamptonartscouncil.org/news/celebrate-the-arts-first-night-northampton-2027-is-coming) is Northampton's alcohol-free, family-friendly New Year's Eve arts festival. It is on Thursday, December 31, and starts at 12:00 PM in downtown Northampton. There are over 100 performances across more than 20 downtown venues, plus fireworks and a midnight countdown.

## A late breakfast before noon

Our regular Thursday hours are 6 a.m. to 2 p.m., and breakfast is served all day. That means a late morning stop works just as well for eggs and pancakes as it does for a sandwich. If you want to be in Northampton when things begin at noon, come in early enough to eat and get on the road.

For a big plate before a long day, the Copperline Special ($12.25) is three eggs, two pancakes, homefries, and toast. The Hash Breakfast ($11.50) is our homemade corned beef hash with two eggs and toast. The Eggs Benedict ($11.50) comes with homefries.

## Bringing the kids

First Night is a family-friendly event. Our Kids' Breakfast ($7.50) is for children under 12. They can choose one egg and toast, two French toast, or two pancakes, with bacon, sausage, or ham, plus milk or juice. We have high chairs and booster seats, and there are crayons and coloring sheets at the table.

We do not take reservations, so seating is first come, first serve. If your group is eight or more, call us at (413) 594-8332 ahead of time so we can get seating ready.

## Something to take along

If you would like food to eat on the way or to have on hand later, call that same number for takeout. Most orders are ready in under 30 minutes. At lunch there is the Reuben ($12.95) and the Turkey Club ($13.25), and breakfast is available to go as well.

We hope your last day of 2026 is a happy one, and we would be glad to see you on your way.
