The [Festival of Trees](https://sbgc.org/fot/) is in its last week. This is the Springfield Boys & Girls Club's 26th annual festival, presented by Beacon Bank, at the MassMutual Center in Springfield. It runs through Sunday, December 13. Check their page for times before you head out.

## Breakfast or lunch first

We serve breakfast all day, and lunch whenever we're open, so you can eat with us at any hour before a trip into Springfield. A few dishes from our regular menu:

- **Eggs Benedict** ($11.50), with our house-made hollandaise
- **Hash Breakfast** ($11.50), our homemade corned beef hash with two eggs and toast
- **Copperline Special** ($12.25), with three eggs, two pancakes, homefries and toast
- **Reuben** ($12.95) or **The Pilgrim** ($11.95), which is turkey with stuffing and cranberry sauce on a water roll

If you're bringing children, the **Kids' Breakfast** is $7.50 for guests under 12. They can choose one egg and toast, two French toast, or two pancakes, with bacon, sausage or ham, plus milk or juice. We have high chairs and booster seats.

Saturday and Sunday mornings between 8 and 11 are usually our busiest. If you want a shorter wait on the weekend, come before 8 or after 11:30. Weekdays are generally quieter. We seat first-come, first-serve, but if you're bringing a group of eight or more, call us at (413) 594-8332 ahead of time and we'll do our best to have seating ready.

## Also this week

- On Sunday, December 13, [Springfield Museums](https://springfieldmuseums.org/organizer/family-programs/) at 21 Edwards Street opens early for Sensory Friendly Sunday, from 9 to 11 a.m. We open at 7 on Sundays, so there's time for breakfast first.
- [Bright Nights at Forest Park](https://www.brightnights.org/) is in its 32nd season of drive-through holiday lights, running through January 3. Tickets must be bought in advance.

Thank you for letting us be part of your December. We hope to see you soon.
