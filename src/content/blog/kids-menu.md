Our children's menu is for guests under 12, and it comes down to one plate, the Kids' Breakfast, for $7.50. Your child picks one egg and toast, two French toast, or two pancakes, then bacon, sausage, or ham to go with it. Milk or juice is included in the price.

Breakfast is served all day here, and the Kids' Breakfast is on our breakfast menu.

For the littlest ones, we have high chairs and booster seats. We also keep crayons and coloring sheets on hand for kids at the table.

If you're bringing the whole family and you'll be eight or more, call us at (413) 594-8332 before you come so we can get seating ready.

Our family has been welcoming local families at 409 Broadway since 1993, and we're always glad to see the next generation pull up a booster seat.
