## Best Breakfast in Western Massachusetts

MassLive readers voted The Copperline Eatery Best Breakfast in Western Massachusetts, and we are proud of that. We are a family-owned diner at 409 Broadway, Chicopee, and recognition like this means a lot to us.

## Our Eggs Benedict

Our Eggs Benedict is two poached eggs on English muffin halves with Canadian bacon, topped with our house-made hollandaise sauce, served with homefries. The hollandaise is the part we hear about most often, and it is the part we take the most care with. We make it in house.

It is $11.50 and comes with homefries.

## Also on the Menu

We are also known for our homemade corned beef hash. The Hash Breakfast is our homemade corned beef hash served with two eggs and toast. You can also order a small or large side of it on its own.

## Find Us

We are open Monday through Friday from 6 a.m. to 2 p.m., Saturday from 6 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., and Sunday from 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. We do not take reservations. High chairs and booster seats are available. We are at 409 Broadway, Chicopee, MA 01020, and you can reach us at (413) 594-8332.
