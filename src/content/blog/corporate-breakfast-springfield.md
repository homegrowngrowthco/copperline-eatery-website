## Morning Events

We have been handling breakfast and brunch catering out of our spot at 409 Broadway in Chicopee since 1993, and we work with groups of all sizes across the area.

## What Breakfast Catering Looks Like

Breakfast and brunch catering is quoted per event. A typical spread includes things like eggs, bacon and sausage, home fries, French toast, and pancakes. The exact mix depends on your group, your budget, and how the morning is structured.

We offer on-site service, hot drop-off, or pick-up at the restaurant.

## A Few Menu Items

People often ask about specific dishes when planning an event. A few things from our regular breakfast menu give a sense of what we build catering around.

Our **Eggs Benedict** ($11.50) is two poached eggs on English muffin halves with Canadian bacon and house-made hollandaise, served with homefries. MassLive readers voted us Best Breakfast in Western Massachusetts, and the hollandaise is part of why.

The **Banana Bread French Toast** ($8.25) uses our homemade banana bread. WWLP 22News recognized us for it on National French Toast Day.

We also offer the **Copperline Special** (three eggs, two pancakes, homefries, and toast) and the **Hash Breakfast** (homemade corned beef hash with two eggs and toast).

These are dine-in menu prices. Catering is priced separately based on your event details.

## Planning Timeline

For most events, we ask for one to two weeks of lead time. For larger groups or weekend dates, plan on three to four weeks.
