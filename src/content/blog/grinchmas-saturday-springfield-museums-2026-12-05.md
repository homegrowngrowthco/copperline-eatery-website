The Springfield Museums are holding [Grinchmas Saturday](https://springfieldmuseums.org/program/grinchmas-saturday-2026/2026-12-05/) on December 5, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. It is a day of hands-on Grinch activities for families, and it is free with museum admission.

If you are taking the kids, breakfast with us first is an easy way to start the day. We open at 6 on Saturdays, so there is time to eat and still be at the museums when the doors open.

The Kids' Breakfast is for children under 12: one egg and toast, two French toast, or two pancakes, with bacon, sausage, or ham, and a milk or juice, for $7.50. We have high chairs and booster seats. Grown-ups have the whole menu, including the Banana Bread French Toast, made from our homemade banana bread.

We do not take reservations. If you are coming as a big group, eight or more, give us a call ahead and we will do our best to fit you in.

## If December 5 does not work

The Grinch is around all season. [Grinch's Grotto](https://springfieldmuseums.org/holiday/) is at the Wood Museum through January 10, and the gingerbread exhibit, Gingerbread: Super Sweet Superheroes, runs through January 3. The museums are closed on Mondays, so check their holiday page for hours before you go.

And if someone on your list loves breakfast, we sell gift cards in any amount, at the register or over the phone.
