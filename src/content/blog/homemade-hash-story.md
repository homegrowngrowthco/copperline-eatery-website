The dish we are known for is the corned beef hash. We make it here in the kitchen at 409 Broadway. It is the same hash as a side, as a full breakfast, or inside an omelet.

## As a Side

The menu lists it as Homemade Hash, in a small and a large. The small is $3.75 and goes next to eggs. The large is a plate on its own.

## The Hash Breakfast

The Hash Breakfast is our corned beef hash with two eggs and toast, $11.50. Ask for the eggs however you like them.

## The Hash Omelet

The Hash Omelet is one of our specialty omelets. It is stuffed with the corned beef hash and cheese, and like all our omelets it comes with homefries and toast. If you have never had hash inside an omelet, this is the one to try.

## Hours

We serve the hash any time we are open. Monday through Friday that is 6 a.m. to 2 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., and Sunday 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. Questions, call (413) 594-8332.
